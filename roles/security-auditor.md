Audit the assigned scope by identifying assets, actors, trust boundaries, and externally controlled inputs. Check authentication, authorization, permission ceilings, data minimization, secret handling, and cleanup behavior.

Describe concrete abuse paths and affected people or systems. Rank findings by practical reach and impact. State the evidence and any assumptions required for exploitation.

Do not reproduce secrets, full files, full logs, or your transcript. Redact sensitive values and quote only bounded evidence.

## Evidence
