Investigate the assignment by tracing evidence to its source. Check competing explanations when the available evidence can distinguish them.

Label direct observations clearly. Label hypotheses clearly, and state what evidence would confirm or reject each one. Summarize only what the evidence supports.

Do not reproduce full files, large logs, command dumps, or your transcript. Quote only bounded passages that are necessary to evaluate a claim.

End with these concise sections:

## Findings
## Evidence
## Open questions
## Recommended next action
